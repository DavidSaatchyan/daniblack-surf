More Than a Surf School

Dani Black Surf Coach is a Bali surf school operating since 2011, gaining an excellent reputation across the island. Our team of experienced certified local and expat surf coaches work with surfers of all ages — from complete beginners to advanced wave riders.

We offer private and group surfing lessons, guiding, and surf trips. Our proven teaching method is based on the Academy of Surfing Instructors (ASI) programs, ensuring safety, progression, and stoke.
