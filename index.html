More Than a Surf School

Dani Black Surf Coach is a Bali surf school operating since 2011 and gained an excellent reputation on the island. Our team of experienced certified local and expat surf coaches work with surfers of all ages.

We offer private and group surfing lessons for beginners, guiding and surf trips for intermediate and advanced surfers of all levels. In our lessons we apply proven consistent teaching method, based on Academy of Surfing Instructors programs (ASI).

Our headquarters located at the front of Canggu, Bali — just steps from the best beginner and intermediate waves.
