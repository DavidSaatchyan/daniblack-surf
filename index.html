Meet Your Coach — Deni Black

Operating since 2011, Deni Black has built an unmatched reputation across Bali. His approach combines technical precision with genuine stoke — because surfing should be both effective and joyful.

Deni works with surfers of all ages and levels, from complete beginners to advanced riders chasing barrels. His teaching method is based on the Academy of Surfing Instructors (ASI) programs, ensuring safety and real progression.

"I believe surfing is about confidence, patience, and reading the ocean. My job is to make you feel safe, understand the waves, and catch that stoke — whether it's your first wave or your hundredth."

— Deni Black, Head Coach

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first wave

Do I need any surfing experience?
Not at all! Most of our students are beginners. We teach you everything from ocean safety to standing on your board. Lessons are tailored to your level.
What should I bring?
Just your towel, sunscreen, and swimwear. We provide surfboards, rash guards, leashes, and drinking water. If you have your own gear — feel free to bring it.
Is it safe for kids?
Absolutely. We have special kids' lessons with instructors in the water at all times. We choose safe, white-water waves perfect for children.
What if the conditions are bad?
We check the surf forecast daily. If conditions aren't safe or suitable, we'll reschedule or suggest a better time. Your safety is our priority.
