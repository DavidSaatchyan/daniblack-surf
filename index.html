More Than a Surf School

Dani Black Surf Coach has been operating in Bali since 2011, building an excellent reputation across the island. Our team of certified local and expat instructors works with surfers of all ages — from first-timers to advanced wave riders.

We offer private lessons, group sessions, and surf guiding. Our teaching method is based on the Academy of Surfing Instructors (ASI) programs, ensuring proven progression and safety in the water.
