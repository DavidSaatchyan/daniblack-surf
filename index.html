More Than a Surf School

Deni Black Surf School has been operating in Bali since 2011, building an excellent reputation across the island. Our team of certified local and expat instructors works with surfers of all ages — from first-timers to advanced wave riders.

We apply proven teaching methods based on the Academy of Surfing Instructors (ASI) programs. Safety, progression, and stoke are at the heart of everything we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Do I need any surfing experience?
Not at all! Most of our students are beginners. We teach you everything from ocean safety to standing on your board.
What should I bring?
Just your towel, sunscreen, and swimwear. We provide surfboards, rash guards, leashes, and drinking water.
Is it safe for kids?
Absolutely. We have special kids' lessons with instructors in the water at all times. Safe, white-water waves.
What if conditions are bad?
We check the surf forecast daily. If conditions aren't safe, we'll reschedule. Your safety is our priority.
